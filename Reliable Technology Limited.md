## How we deliver

A clear process, so you always know what happens next.

1. **Discovery and consultation**We learn your business, goals, audience, and expectations.
2. **Research and planning**Requirements become a plan with defined scope, deliverables, and timelines.
3. **Design and development**We build the agreed solution for usability, performance, and accessibility.
4. **Testing and quality assurance**Functionality, responsiveness, and compatibility are checked before delivery.
5. **Deployment and handover**We launch, document, and show you how to run it.
6. **Maintenance and improvement**Where agreed, ongoing support and updates as your needs change.
